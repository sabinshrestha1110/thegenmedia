About Us

Gen Media Institution is a forward-thinking digital platform committed to empowering individuals with cutting-edge skills and modern technology. We inspire creativity, innovation, and lifelong learning through practical, industry-focused education. Our mission is to bridge the gap between knowledge and opportunity, helping learners grow into confident, skilled professionals ready to thrive in today's fast-paced digital world.

The Story Behind Gen Media

Founded with a single camera and a boundless vision, Gen Media started as a small project in Pathari, Nepal. What began as a passion for visual storytelling has grown into a full-service production house serving brands across Nepal and beyond.

Our team of creatives, strategists, and technologists come together to produce work that doesn't just look great — it performs. Every frame we capture, every story we tell, is guided by our belief: great media changes minds.

Why Choose Us

We bring more than cameras — we bring clarity, strategy, and craftsmanship.

Graphic Designing

Our Graphic Designing course helps students learn creative design skills used in branding, advertising, social media, and digital marketing. Students gain hands-on experience with industry-standard tools while learning logo design, poster creation, photo editing, typography, and visual communication techniques.

Motion Graphics

The Motion Graphics course focuses on creating engaging animated visuals for advertisements, reels, presentations, and digital content. Students learn animation principles, text animation, transitions, visual effects, and professional editing techniques used in modern media production.

Animation

Our Animation course teaches students how to bring ideas and characters to life through creative storytelling and movement. From basic animation principles to advanced character animation and scene creation, students develop practical skills used in films, gaming, advertising, and digital entertainment.

Practical Learning Experience

We focus on practical, project-based learning to help students build real-world experience. Students work on creative assignments and industry-style projects that improve both technical and creative skills.

Career-Focused Training

Our courses are designed to prepare students for freelance work, content creation, media industries, and professional creative careers. We provide guidance that helps students build confidence, creativity, and industry-ready skills.
